05

The human gate

The decision is on the recommended action — never acceptance of a shortage. On a SHORT_UNCOVERED or INSUFFICIENT_DATA line, approval is not offered at all, and the engine refuses it even if asked directly. A documented procurement need stays documented whatever happens on other lines.

Decision log

Every decision is logged with its time, and can be changed or undone. The log is saved in this browser only (same pattern as the V2 planner).

The architecture — BTP design, on a screen

This dashboard is the SAP Business AI Platform pattern, applied to a real MRO decision: events wake the agents, context tells them what things mean, guardrails decide what they are allowed to do, and a human owns the commitments.

L5

Human gate

Approve or reject at the gate, logged with time, changeable. No agent commits stock. The human owns the commitment.

L4

Guardrails

Approval never applies to a SHORT_UNCOVERED or INSUFFICIENT_DATA line. A documented procurement need is never suppressed by a covered line elsewhere (E006/P011 principle).
