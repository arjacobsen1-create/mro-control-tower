0Decided

Decisions are made by the planner in the planner screen — and the decision is on the recommended action, not the verdict. A covered line can be approved for its coverage action; a short or insufficient-data line offers escalation only, never approval — a shortage cannot be accepted. This dashboard starts clean: nothing has been decided yet, all 10 are waiting.

06

Stage 6 fixes — verified

Two wording problems found in the first evaluation, fixed in code, then proven by re-running the full evaluation. Both fixes held at 10/10.

1 Day-counts computed by code, not the model

Day differences are now computed by the rule engine, never estimated by the model. The scenario that once produced a wrong figure now states the exact code-computed gap.

Before — model said“miss by 227 days”
Invented number, wrong.
→
After — engine computes265 days after event start
Exact, from the dates.

2 Citation check on every recommendation

A policy-driven recommendation must cite the note it relies on. If the citation is missing, an automatic check catches it and retries — in the Stage 6 run it fired once and fixed a missing citation before the result was recorded.
