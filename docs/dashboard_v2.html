Slippage risk — per (line, PO)

Slippage attaches to the line, not the PO. Of the 6 (line, PO) pairs where a PO actually carries a line's coverage, 2 are HIGH risk and 1 is on watch at a 21-day margin (WATCH_MARGIN_DAYS = 21 — three weeks being the minimum realistic window to react to a slipping PO). A pair only flags if the PO is load-bearing for that line and a repeat of its observed slip would break the margin.

07

Horizon — two clocks, forward events

With lead times of 450–540 days on the long-lead parts, a flag raised weeks before an event is a last-minute alarm, not an early warning. The horizon model therefore runs on two clocks — and it is also why forward events had to exist: on the original six events every line sits inside three months, so neither clock has anything to discriminate.

Clock 1 — order-by (procurement warning)

≤ 6 mo
Advisory ≤ 3 mo
Act-now

Driven by each line's order-by date. Advisory when the order-by date is within 6 months, act-now inside 3 months, escalate when it has passed with no counting PO in place. This clock is what separates the 2 lines where an order decision is still losable from the 31 where that door has closed.

Clock 2 — event (coverage & customer warning)

6 & 3 mo
Scheduled reviews ≥ 180 d
Long-lead line

Every long-lead line (lead time ≥ 180 days — 22 of the 33) gets a scheduled coverage review at 6 and 3 months before its event, covered or not, because a covered position can deteriorate. An exception or a customer advisory is raised only on an identified material risk condition — a post-allocation shortage, a contended shortage, a HIGH slippage pair, or counted-PO coverage with no ordering fallback.

The human gate — V2

The agent recommends. A person decides. These are the 15 assessed lines of the V2 evaluation, waiting in the V2 planner screen.

15Assessed lines
0Decided
0Escalated
15Pending

Decisions are made by the planner in the V2 planner screen — and the decision is on the recommended action, never on the shortage itself. A short or insufficient-data line offers escalation only; a SLIPPAGE_HIGH line cannot be passively monitored, and every HIGH decision records its recovery path. Customer notification is a prepared draft, never sent: release requires the planner to verify the affected event, the material shortfall, the recovery options and the proposed communication. And an OK verdict does not suppress a documented procurement need — a covered line can still carry its own order decision.

10

Stage fixes — what V2 broke, and what changed

Three findings from the V2 pass and its design decisions — fixed in the open or held on the record, none smoothed over. Sources: build/build_log.md and the V2 addendum to evals/evaluation_report.md.

1 Run failures — loud, not wrong

R01 and X02a produced no assessment at all. On E001 / P002 — short in neither view — get_horizon returns recovery.feasible: null, and the model intermittently copied that null into the boolean feasible_recovery_exists field, so validation rejected the run. The fault was in the agent layer only: the engines and the ground truth were never wrong.

After — agent layer onlyPrompt rule + validation retry
The null named in the prompt, asserts carrying messages, one retry with the failure named — verified 5/5 on E001 / P002. Score of record held at 18/20.

2 One line, two primary decisions

E006 / P011 was assessed three times (C03, W02, P01). Twice the recommended decision was escalate_expedite; once it was place_order. Both are real decisions on different dimensions of the same line — but §7 precedence puts the shortage row first, so place_order is the weaker reading. No scored field was affected, and the procurement action was raised in all three runs.

3 The D2 correction — 21 days changed nothing

Widening the watch margin from the recommended 14 to the decided 21 days (WATCH_MARGIN_DAYS) was expected to bring E004 / P021 — margin 19 — into the watch band. Recomputed in code, it changes zero flags on current data: E004's site stock (8) covers its requirement (1) with or without PO-1012, so that PO is not load-bearing, and the load-bearing condition excludes the pair at any threshold. The correction stands in the spec (§4.2 recomputation note); the wider band binds on future data.
